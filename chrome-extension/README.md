# 🚀 GetSetMart E-Commerce Seller Assistant (Custom Chrome Extension)

This is your official custom **GetSetMart Chrome Extension** for 1-Click Meesho, Flipkart, and Amazon catalog listing autofill, thermal 4x6 label cropping, and order synchronization!

---

## 📥 How to Install in Google Chrome (10-Second Setup):

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your computer.
2. Type `chrome://extensions` in your browser address bar and press **Enter**.
3. In the top-right corner, toggle **ON** the switch for **Developer mode**.
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** (अनपैक लोड करें) button at the top-left.
5. Select the folder:
   `c:\Users\sachi\OneDrive\Desktop\GET SET MART\chrome-extension`
6. Click **Select Folder**.

🎉 **Done!** The **GetSetMart Seller Assistant** icon will appear in your Chrome extensions bar!

---

## 🔥 Features:

1. **⚡ 1-Click Listing Autofill**:
   - Open Meesho / Flipkart / Amazon seller panel.
   - Click the extension icon, choose your product template (e.g. *Oversized Hoodie*, *Anarkali Suit*, *Kundan Jewelry*), and click **"⚡ 1-Click Autofill Listing Form"**.
   - HSN codes, GST rates, titles, and descriptions fill automatically!

2. **✂️ Quick 4x6 Thermal Label Cropper**:
   - 1-Click opens `getsetmart.com/admin/label-cropper` for instant A4 to 4x6 thermal PDF label cropping.

3. **🔄 Live Order Sync**:
   - Syncs marketplace orders to your `getsetmart.com/admin` ERP Dashboard.
